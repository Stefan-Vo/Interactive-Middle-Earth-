# The named people of Dor-lómin (FA 468), in the order the map expects. The Edain of Hador's house were tall Men, golden-haired; Morwen and Túrin were dark-haired, of the House of Bëor's looks.
Húrin Thalion, Lord of Dor-lómin: a broad, strong human warrior of middle height with golden hair to the shoulders and a short golden beard, bright chain mail, a dark green cloak with a gold clasp, a long sword and a round shield
Morwen Eledhwen, Lady of Dor-lómin: a tall, stern human woman with long dark hair, grey eyes, a dark blue and silver gown, a grey cloak, unarmed
Túrin as a small boy: a small human child with dark hair, grey eyes, a plain green tunic, brown boots and a small wooden toy sword
Lalaith, a little girl: a tiny human child with bright golden curly hair, a pale yellow dress, barefoot, laughing, unarmed
Sador Labadal, an old lame servant: an old thin human man with grey hair and a grey beard, a brown woodworker's smock, a wooden peg foot, leaning on a crutch, a small carving knife
Huor son of Galdor: a tall young human man with long golden hair and no beard, chain mail under a green and gold surcoat, a long sword
